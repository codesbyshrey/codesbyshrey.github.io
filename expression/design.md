# Expression Page Design Notes

## Current Purpose

The expression page combines Practice Intuition, Somanaut, contact links, public writing, social links, selected work, and product documents. It currently absorbs the earlier connect page and acts as the public-facing communication hub.

This page is less resume-like than Experiences and less credential-focused than Education. It should communicate the philosophy, writing, product direction, and ways to connect.

## Current Visual Approach

The expression page uses:
- A simple hero
- Two primary panels for philosophy/application
- Repeated rows of link cards
- A bottom row grouping selected work, documents, and conversation areas
- Dropdowns for Bonita and Somanaut details because those are secondary to the main page purpose

Current category color language:
- Red: tech stack / education-coded items
- Blue: concepts, themes, frameworks, experience tags
- Purple: live links and internal reference links

## Design Intent So Far

The current page is intended as the communication and philosophy hub:
- Practice Intuition is the conceptual umbrella.
- Somanaut is the application layer.
- Bonita is the proof-of-concept MVP that came before Somanaut.
- Connect/contact links are merged here rather than living as a separate top-level page.

## Future Edit Notes

Future edits may:
- Split Somanaut into its own page if it becomes too large
- Make Practice Intuition visually more editorial
- Create a stronger writing archive or featured writing section
- Turn the social/contact rows into a more intentional contact section
- Remove dropdowns if the page becomes a more direct one-page communication hub
