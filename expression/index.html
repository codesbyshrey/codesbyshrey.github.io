Practice Intuition

Process philosophy and think tank.

Practice Intuition is the writing, research, and design philosophy behind the work. It is where I study adaptive human systems, embodied learning, trust, attention, behavior, and the conditions that let people express intelligence without obstruction.

  • Frameworks for learning, movement, health, and technology.
  • Essays and research notes on how people adapt across real contexts.
  • A think tank for products where trust, safety, behavior, and outcomes matter.

Somanaut

Integrated application.

Somanaut is the application layer: a coaching product designed to help your mind better operate your brain-body interface. It turns the Practice Intuition philosophy into protocols, reflection loops, adaptive guidance, and practical self-coaching.

  • Brain-body interface exploration for attention, physiology, and behavior.
  • Structured coaching flows that connect reflection with action.
  • A product system for learning how to become your own coach.
Bonita - PoC MVP

Bonita was the proof-of-concept MVP that came before Somanaut. It tested wellness intelligence, structured triage, protocol generation, and safety boundaries before the direction expanded into a broader coaching application.

Somanaut system direction

Somanaut is the broader product direction that Bonita helped test: a self-coaching application for translating body signals, attention, reflection, and behavior into practical protocols.

Input

Reflection, symptoms, habits, training context, emotional state, and daily constraints.

Processing

Brain-body interface framing, protocol routing, safety boundaries, and follow-up loops.

Output

Actionable self-coaching steps that help users understand what to notice and what to do next.
