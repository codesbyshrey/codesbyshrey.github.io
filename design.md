# Home Page Design Notes

## Current Purpose

The home page is the broadest portfolio entry point. It introduces Shreyas as a human-centered product engineer working across clinical AI, human factors, biomedical engineering, full-stack prototyping, biomechanics, and human performance systems.

The current design is intentionally structured as a map of the portfolio rather than a traditional landing page. It gives visitors a quick way to understand the three portfolio lenses, the education/performance split, and the personal lenses that inform the work.

## Current Visual Approach

The home page uses a wireframe-like visual structure:
- Thin bordered boxes
- Minimal decorative styling
- Clear route groupings
- Large functional sections instead of marketing hero imagery
- Dense but scannable layout

The page is currently optimized for content organization and editability before heavier visual design is added.
