gainsbyshrey

Coaching, biomechanics, and the human body as an adaptive system.

gainsbyshrey is the physical training and coaching portfolio: personal training, swim instruction, biomechanics, EMT context, biomedical engineering, and the practical work of helping people understand the body as an adaptive system.

People

I work well with people who need patient coaching: beginners, fearful swimmers, athletes, patients relearning movement, and clients who need technical clarity without being overwhelmed.

Product

The offer is coaching and physical training with a technical eye: strength, mobility, movement literacy, body awareness, and practical systems for becoming your own coach.

Problem

People often know they want to move better, feel stronger, or hurt less, but they do not know what their body is actually telling them or what small change to make next.

Process

I use observation, movement cues, biomechanics, scope-aware education, and repeated feedback to help clients understand the relationship between attention, position, breath, force, and behavior.

Objective

As a coach and biomechanics-focused trainer, I want to help people build strength, confidence, movement literacy, and body awareness through practical coaching that respects physiology, psychology, scope, and lived context.
